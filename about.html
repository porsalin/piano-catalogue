About

Scope

Two separate catalogues: Solo (keyboard alone) and Concertos (piano with orchestra). Rows are never mixed. Blanks mean unverified — nothing is guessed.

This build

  • Solo: 195 pianists · 3,735 recordings · 350 composers
  • Concertos: 57 composers · 3,458 recordings · 384 pianists

Notes

Solo excludes concertos and chamber; Alkan Op. 39 solo works belong in Solo. Static HTML — no paid APIs.
